Get to Know Us!

Three students, one idea ♡

BUSINESS MANAGEMENT • GROUP 2

Hello and welcome to MOCHI TOTEBAGS! We are a team of three students participating in an extracurricular Business Management program. As part of our learning experience, we came up with the idea of creating MOCHI TOTEBAGS, a small business that focuses on cute, stylish, and practical tote bags for everyday use. Through this project, we aim to learn more about entrepreneurship, teamwork, creativity, marketing, and how to manage a business. MOCHI TOTEBAGS represents our creativity, collaboration, and passion for turning a simple idea into something meaningful. Thank you for supporting our little business! ♡
